---
title: "Performance"
---

The **Performance** module focuses on campaign effectiveness and media cost-efficiency. This dashboard provides deep insights into estimated audience exposure (Impressions) and evaluates the relative financial efficiency required to reach that audience through Cost Per Mille (CPM) analysis.

<Info>
  Impression and CPM figures are estimations based on location traffic data and industry standard.
</Info>

Here is a detailed breakdown of each section within the Performance tab:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Estimated Total Impression Comparison">
    This interactive line chart tracks the estimated audience exposure (impressions) over time for each selected brand. It helps you quickly identify seasonal traffic trends and periods of high visibility, such as major campaign pushes.

    ![Performance Estimated Total Impression Comparison](/images/performance-estimated-total-impression-comparison.png)

    While the chart defaults to a monthly view for granular tracking, you can easily use the dropdown menu in the top right corner to adjust the timeframe to a Quarterly, Semester, or Yearly view for a broader macro-analysis of competitor reach.

    ![Performance Estimated Total Impression Comparison Filter](/images/performance-estimated-total-impression-comparison-filter.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Estimated Brand Impression Details">
    This section displays a detailed matrix of the estimated audience impressions generated by each brand on a monthly basis.

    ![Performance Estimated Brand Impression Details](/images/performance-estimated-brand-impression-details.png)

    This table features an interactive drill-down capability; by clicking the plus (\+) icon next to a brand's name, you can expand the row to view highly granular data.

    ![Performance Estimated Brand Impression Details Expand](/images/performance-estimated-brand-impression-details-expand.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Average CPM by City">
    This grouped bar chart displays the estimated Cost Per Mille (CPM) across various geographical locations, segmented by Conventional and Digital inventory formats. It serves as an essential tool for evaluating regional media pricing, allowing you to instantly identify which cities offer the most cost-effective audience reach and where you are likely to pay a premium for visibility.

    ![Performance Average Cpm By City](/images/performance-average-cpm-by-city.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Average CPM by Brand">
    This horizontal bar chart compares the overall cost-efficiency among your selected competitors by displaying their average CPM. By breaking down the data into Conventional and Digital formats, it reveals how effectively each brand is converting its budget into actual impressions. This visualization helps you determine if a competitor is highly optimizing their media buying strategy for lower costs or if they are actively choosing to pay a premium rate to secure specific high-value placements.

    ![Performance Cpm By Brand](/images/performance-cpm-by-brand.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Estimated CPM Details">
    This section provides a highly detailed tabular matrix that breaks down the estimated Cost Per Mille (CPM) for each selected brand across different geographical locations. It cross-references the Cities (on the Y-axis) with the Brands (on the X-axis) to display the exact Rupiah value required to generate one thousand impressions in that specific market.

    ![Performance Estimated Cpm Details](/images/performance-estimated-cpm-details.png)

    <Info>
      To ensure accurate cost analysis, you can filter this table by specific media formats using the dropdown menu located in the top right corner of the table card.
    </Info>
  </Step>
</Steps>
