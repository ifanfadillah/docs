title: "Taxonomy V1"
---

This page defines the fundamental classification standards and rules applied to the Playlog Insight (Taxonomy V1). Adhering to these rules ensures data consistency and accuracy across the Insight module, enabling reliable competitor analysis.

These classifications are applied through strict manual entry processes to ensure that what is shown in the advertisement is accurately represented in the database.

## Sub Industry Category & IDs

### 01 - Food & Grocery

| ID | Sub Industry Name | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **01.01** | **Bakery & Baking Products** | Ingredients for making bread and cakes (and all other related items) in solid and liquid forms. Examples: Live yeast, raisins, TBM/Ovallet, food coloring, cake essence, cake flour. |
| **01.02** | **Breakfast Food** | Food for breakfast, including accompaniments eaten with bread, excluding margarine. Examples: Bread, jam, sprinkles (meisjes), cereal, cereal milk. |
| **01.03** | **Snacks (Include Biscuits, Cookies, Chips)** | Light meals or snacks usually eaten between meals. Examples: Cake, layer cake (lapis legit), biscuits, nuts, wafers, sunflower seeds (kwaci), popcorn, dodol. Also includes: Side dishes for main meals (some can be eaten directly, some require cooking first). Examples: Meat floss (abon), chips/crackers (kerupuk, emping, etc.). |
| **01.04** | **Confectionary Food** | Snacks made predominantly from cocoa/chocolate beans, usually in bar or round shapes, sometimes with added nuts, biscuits, or wafers as complements. Examples: Chocolate bars, chocolate wafers. Also includes: All candies of various flavors (sweet, spicy, sour), including chewing gum, chocolate-flavored candies, and breath mints. Examples: Candy, chewing gum. |
| **01.05** | **Cooking & Flavour Oil** | Food ingredients intended for frying (in liquid form), derived from various sources (coconut, corn, etc.). Examples: Cooking oil, sunflower oil, corn oil, vegetable oil, coconut oil, etc. Also includes: Food ingredients used as flavor enhancers, typically for salads. Examples: Mayonnaise, salad dressing, French dressing, Thousand Island dressing. |
| **01.06** | **Seasoning & Condiments** | Food seasonings added to food as flavor enhancers (in solid, liquid, powder, or flour form), including soy sauce and sauces (chili or tomato). Examples: Chili, soy sauce, bouillon, bouillon cubes, salt/MSG. |
| **01.07** | **Fresh & Frozen Food** | Food derived from fruits or vegetables that are fresh, dried, or frozen, and have not gone through canning or other processes. Examples: Fresh fruits, fresh vegetables. |
| **01.08** | **Canned Foods** | Food derived from fresh, dried, or frozen fruits or vegetables packaged in special cans. Examples: Canned fruits, vegetables, etc. Also includes: Food (meat, fish, fruit, etc.) that has been processed or prepared, then packaged in cans or special containers. Examples: Canned fish/meat, sausages, corned beef, etc. |
| **01.09** | **Instant Food** | Fast food that requires a short cooking time or simply adding hot water. In a hard or solid state before cooking and soft after cooking. Examples: Noodles, soup, macaroni, spaghetti, instant chicken porridge. |
| **01.10** | **Diet & Health Foods** | Foods with special formulas (low calorie) intended for those trying to lose or prevent excess weight (such as high cholesterol or blood sugar levels), maintain body health, stability, and immunity. Examples: Diet sugar/low-calorie sweetener, diet cookies, diet food, protein food, grainy food. |
| **01.11** | **Range of Food Products** | This category is intended for ad creatives that simultaneously display various categories within a single creative. |
| **01.12** | **Other Food Products** | Other types of food products not included in the sub-industries above. |
