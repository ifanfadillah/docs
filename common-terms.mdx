---
title: "Common Terms"
---

This glossary provides definitions for the key terminologies, metrics, and features used across the Playlog platform to help you navigate your media planning and measurement effectively.

## A

**Angle to Road**: The exact degree at which the screen faces oncoming traffic, which determines how directly the audience can view the advertisement.

**Avg. Impression/day**: The standard baseline metric showing the average daily audience exposures the screen typically generates.

---

## B

**Billboard**: A specific physical advertising structure or screen, which can be formatted as a Digital Billboard (DOOH) or a Conventional Billboard (OOH).

---

## C

**Convert to Campaign**: Triggering the workflow to transform your finalized media plan directly into an active, live campaign within the platform.

**Coverage**: This section provides an overview of how wide the reach is for your competitor's Out-of-Home (OOH) or Digital Out-of-Home (DOOH) campaigns.

**CPM (Cost Per Mille / eCPM)**: The metric that displays the estimated cost required to reach one thousand impressions. It is a crucial metric for evaluating the cost-efficiency of the inventory. To calculate the Cost Per Mille, the standard formula used is:

$$CPM=\left(\frac{\text{Total Cost}}{\text{Total Impressions}}\right)\times1000$$.

---

## D

**Density Map**: A color-coded grid system covering the map so you can visually analyze the audience density surrounding specific ad placements.

**Direction Facing**: The compass direction the front of the screen is pointing toward. When viewing the map cone, the direction the cone points reveals exactly which way the front of the screen is facing.

---

## E

**Export Proposal**: Once you are satisfied with the grouped inventory in your Shortlist, you can easily export this curated list into a comprehensive, professional media proposal.

---

## F

**Frequency**: The average number of times the ads are seen by the unique population reached.

---

## I

**Impression**: The total number of exposures to your advertisement. This metric interacts with Reach and Frequency to determine the total campaign delivery, calculated simply as Reach x Frequency = Impression.

**Inventory Audit**: The essential first step in the pre-methodology framework. It involves thoroughly identifying the physical specifications and geospatial context of the advertising asset.

---

## M

**Maximum Noting Distance**: The farthest distance at which a person can effectively perceive and comprehend the content displayed on a billboard. The core formula used to determine this relies on the physical dimensions of the screen and the viewer's angle of sight:

$$\frac{(height+\sqrt{width^2+length^2})/2}{\tan(\min\theta)}$$.

---

## O

**OOH Media Measurement**: Out-of-Home (OOH) media measurement is the industry standard for quantifying the audience exposed to physical advertising assets.

---

## P

**Peak Impression**: The highest traffic point or maximum number of exposures achieved, helping you identify the absolute busiest times for a location.

**Pointer (Inventory Pin)**: Each pointer represents the exact physical geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) of a billboard structure on the map.

**Primary Data**: Involves direct, active signals collected to measure audience movement around the specific viewing zone of a physical screen.

**Proof of Play**: A real-world photo of that exact advertisement playing on the physical screen. This visual evidence provides absolute confidence in the screen's visibility and quality.

---

## R

**Rate Card (Price Estimation)**: This provides the estimated baseline cost to rent the billboard for a specific duration, such as a monthly rate.

**Reach**: The number of unique individuals that will likely see your ads regardless of frequency.

---

## S

**Secondary Data**: Utilizes existing, macro-level datasets to establish baseline audience models, enrich demographic insights, and validate the primary data.

**Shortlist**: Functions as a dedicated storage or selection list for the inventory you are interested in while exploring the platform. Grouping selected inventory into a Shortlist makes it significantly easier to convert them into an active media plan.

**Share of Voice (SOV)**: A metric dictated by the number of advertisement slots you secure on a digital screen's rotation cycle; securing a slot on a screen with fewer maximum slots means your advertisement will cycle back and appear more frequently, granting you a higher Share of Voice.

**Slot (Max Slot)**: This refers to the total number of advertisement slots available within the digital screen's rotation.

**Spending**: This section focuses on the estimated budget spent by brands or competitors across various locations.

**Spot**: A spot represents the actual, individual instance of your advertisement playing on the screen.

---

## T

**Taxonomy V1**: The fundamental classification standards and rules applied to the Playlog Insight module. Adhering to these rules ensures data consistency and accuracy across the Insight module, enabling reliable competitor analysis.

**Total Spot per slot**: The number of times your ad will play within a given period\[cite: 1033\].

**Traffic Heatmap**: An overlay of general traffic condition data onto the map, helping you understand the typical vehicle flow around potential billboard locations.

---

## U

**Unit (Total Unit)**: This indicates the total number of physical screens available at a specific location.

---

## V

**Video Duration per spot**: The length of time each individual ad plays, such as 30 seconds.

**Viewing Zone**: When you click on a specific inventory pointer on the map, a transparent blue cone or polygon projects outward from the pin, representing a highly detailed visualization of the billboard's "Direction Facing" and its actual "Viewing Zone."
