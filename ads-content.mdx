---
title: "Ads Content"
---

The **Ads Content** section acts as a visual library of currently industry or brand running ads on billboard, while also serving as a practical tool for planning your own campaigns.

Here is a detailed breakdown of the section available in the Ads Content tab:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Billboard List">
    This table provides a summary of aggregated data for each competitor brand.

    ![Ads Content Billboard List](/images/ads-content-billboard-list.png)

    By clicking the plus (\+) icon to the left of the brand name, you can expand the row to view individual inventory details.

    ![Ads Content Billboard List Expand](/images/ads-content-billboard-list-expand.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="View Ads">
    This section acts as a visual library of currently running ads, while also serving as a practical tool for planning your own campaigns. By accessing this feature, you can directly monitor the relevant visual design and messaging strategies utilized by your competitors.

    ![Ads Content View List 1 2](/images/ads-content-view-list-1-2.png)

    ![Ads Content View List 2](/images/ads-content-view-list-2.png)

    ![Ads Content View List 3](/images/ads-content-view-list-3.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Convert to Shortlist">
    This feature bridges your competitor research directly with your campaign planning workflow.

    <Accordion title="How it Work?">
      <Steps>
        <Step title='Step 1: Click "Action" Button'>
          ![Ads Content Convert To Shortlist 1](/images/ads-content-convert-to-shortlist-1.png)
        </Step>
        <Step title='Step 2: Click "Convert to Shortlist"'>
          ![Ads Content Convert To Shortlist 2](/images/ads-content-convert-to-shortlist-2.png)
        </Step>
        <Step title="Step 3: Select Billboard">
          ![Ads Content Convert To Shortlist 3](/images/ads-content-convert-to-shortlist-3.png)
        </Step>
        <Step title='Step 4: Click "Save" to Continue Select Shortlist'>
          ![Ads Content Convert To Shortlist 4](/images/ads-content-convert-to-shortlist-4.png)
        </Step>
        <Step title='Step 5: Browse or search for your pre-planned Shortlist (e.g., "Shortlist A")'>
          ![Ads Content Convert To Shortlist 5](/images/ads-content-convert-to-shortlist-5.png)
        </Step>
        <Step title="Step 6: Finalize Your Convert List Billboard to Shortlist">
          ![Ads Content Convert To Shortlist 6](/images/ads-content-convert-to-shortlist-6.png)
        </Step>
      </Steps>
    </Accordion>

    <Accordion title="Can't find your Shortlist? Create a new one here!">
      If you haven't created a Shortlist yet, or need a new workspace for this specific inventory, you can create it directly from this modal without losing your progress:

      <Steps>
        <Step title="Click the + Create New button located at the top right of the Shortlist modal.">
          ![Create New Shortlist From Modal Ads Content 1](/images/create-new-shortlist-from-modal-ads-content-1.png)
        </Step>
        <Step title="A new form will appear. Enter your desired Shortlist Name and an optional Description to help you easily identify the campaign later.">
          ![Create New Shortlist From Modal Ads Content 2](/images/create-new-shortlist-from-modal-ads-content-2.png)
        </Step>
        <Step title="Click the Save button to finalize.">
          ![Create New Shortlist From Modal Ads Content 3](/images/create-new-shortlist-from-modal-ads-content-3.png)
        </Step>
      </Steps>
    </Accordion>
  </Step>
