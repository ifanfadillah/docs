title: "What is Insight"
---

![Cover Insight](/images/cover-insight.png)

In the Playlog ecosystem, the **Insight** module serves as the central hub for competitive intelligence and market analysis for media buyers, agencies, and direct advertisers. Because Playlog operates as a strategic planning and execution platform, this module is designed to provide comprehensive visibility into competitor OOH and DOOH campaigns, empowering you to make data-driven decisions before launching your own.

Through this module, users can explore real-world competitor data broken down into four core pillars:

- **Coverage:** Monitor the geographical reach and inventory distribution (Conventional vs. Digital) of competitor campaigns.
- **Spending:** Track estimated budget allocations and historical ad spend trends across different brands and cities.
- **Performance:** Evaluate the estimated market exposure, audience impressions, and CPM efficiency of running campaigns.
- **Ads Content:** Browse a visual library of active ad creatives and seamlessly convert strategic competitor inventory into a **Shortlist** for your own future media planning.
