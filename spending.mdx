---
title: "Spending"
---

The **Spending** section focuses on financial intelligence. This dashboard provides deep insights into the historical & estimated budgets your competitors are allocating to their OOH and DOOH campaigns. It allows you to analyze where they are investing the most money and how their financial strategies shift over time.

<Info>
  The spending figures presented in this module are estimated based on industry rate cards and tracked inventory usage.
</Info>

Here is a detailed breakdown of each section within the Spending:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Total Ad Spend">
    The overarching estimated budget for all campaigns currently visible in your filtered view. It represents the combined financial investment across all selected brands, locations, and inventory types.

    ![Spending Total Ad Spend](/images/spending-total-ad-spend.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Total Ad Spend OOH">
    The financial data to show the estimated budget allocated exclusively to Conventional (static) Out-of-Home placements.

    ![Spending Total Ad Spend Ooh](/images/spending-total-ad-spend-ooh.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Total Ad Spend DOOH">
    The estimated budget is directed specifically towards Digital Out-of-Home screens.

    ![Spending Total Ad Spend Dooh](/images/spending-total-ad-spend-dooh.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spending by City">
    This stacked bar chart illustrates the absolute total estimated budget allocated to each specific city within your filtered view. By breaking down the total regional investment into Conventional (static) and Digital (DOOH) expenditures using color-coded bars, this visualization makes it easy to instantly identify which geographical markets are receiving the heaviest financial focus from your competitors.

    ![Spending By City](/images/spending-by-city.png)

    For example, you can quickly see if a competitor is pouring billions into digital screens in Central Jakarta while relying on cheaper conventional boards in other regions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Average Spending by City">
    While the previous chart shows the total budget, this grouped bar chart displays the _average_ estimated cost per ad placement within each city. This is a crucial metric for understanding regional pricing disparities and market premiums.

    ![Avg Spending By City](/images/avg-spending-by-city.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spending Comparison">
    This pie chart provides a clear visual breakdown of the "Share of Wallet" among your selected competitors. It calculates the percentage of the total estimated budget attributed to each brand, allowing you to instantly grasp the financial market share. This provides a quick snapshot of which competitor holds the most purchasing power and dominance within your filtered criteria.

    ![Spending Comparison](/images/spending-comparison.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spending By Brands">
    This horizontally stacked bar chart ranks your competitor brands based on their absolute total estimated financial investment. Crucially, it dissects each brand's overarching budget by showing exactly how much capital is allocated to Conventional (static) inventory versus Digital (DOOH) inventory.

    ![Spending By Brands](/images/spending-by-brands.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spending Coverage by City">
    This section presents a detailed financial heatmap matrix. Instead of showing the physical count of billboards, this table displays the exact estimated monetary value spent by each brand (listed on the Y-axis) across different cities (listed on the X-axis).

    ![Spending Coverage By City](/images/spending-coverage-by-city.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spending SOV (Share of Voice)">
    This 100% stacked bar chart illustrates financial dominance and market share over time. Unlike the physical Share of Voice chart in the Coverage tab, this visualization tracks the percentage of the _total market spend_ attributed to each brand on a month-by-month basis.

    ![Spending Sov](/images/spending-sov.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expenses Comparison">
    This line chart visualizes the estimated spending trajectory for each selected brand over time. It is an essential tool for tracking financial momentum, identifying seasonal budget spikes (e.g., massive holiday spending), or noticing when a competitor suddenly scales back their media investment.

    ![Expenses Comparison](/images/expenses-comparison.png)

    <Info>
      By default, this chart displays data on a **Monthly** basis to show granular, month-to-month fluctuations. However, you can dynamically adjust the time aggregation using the dropdown menu located in the top right corner of the chart card.
    </Info>

    ![Expenses Comparison Filter](/images/expenses-comparison-filter.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Estimated Brand Expenses ">
    This section provides a highly granular, tabular matrix containing the exact estimated monetary figures behind your competitors' financial strategies. It displays the estimated expenditure for each brand broken down month by month, alongside a final annual total.

    ![Spending Estimated Brand Expenses](/images/spending-estimated-brand-expenses.png)

    <Info>
      Initially, the table lists the selected competitor brands on the left, showing their total estimated monthly spending. A "Total Expenses" row at the bottom calculates the combined monthly spend of all brands in the view.
    </Info>

    <Accordion title="How to Expand Detail Estimated Brand Expenses">
      By clicking the **plus (\+)** icon next to any brand name, you can expand its row to reveal the most granular financial data available. This breakdown shows exactly how much budget that specific brand allocated to individual, named billboard locations (e.g., specific LED screens on major roads) for each specific month.

      ![Spending Estimated Brand Expenses 2](/images/spending-estimated-brand-expenses-2.png)
    </Accordion>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Total Expenses by Billboard Type">
    This stacked bar chart visualizes the overarching market's financial investment over time, specifically broken down by inventory format. Rather than looking at individual brands, it tracks the combined monthly expenditure of all your selected competitors throughout the year. Each monthly bar is split to reveal exactly how much of that period's overall budget was dedicated to **Conventional** (static) boards versus **Digital** (DOOH) screens.

    ![Spending Total Expenses](/images/spending-total-expenses.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>
