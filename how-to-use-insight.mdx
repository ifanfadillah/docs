This guide provides a step-by-step workflow on how to navigate the Playlog Insight dashboard, from initially accessing the feature to configuring your filters and dynamically analyzing competitor data.

## Step 1: Accessing Insight

You can access the campaign feature by clicking the ‘**Insight**’ Menu in the Navigation Bar.

![How To Use Insight 1](/images/how-to-use-insight-1.png)

Then it will display the **Insight Page.**

![How To Use Insight 1 2](/images/how-to-use-insight-1.2.png)

When you navigate to the **Insight** menu for the first time, the dashboard will display a set of placeholder data by default.

<Info>
  You will see a banner at the top of the interface stating: _"You are showing a sample view. Apply filters to see your real competitor analysis."_
</Info>

<Info>
  This sample view allows you to familiarize yourself with the layout, charts, and available metrics before diving into your actual competitive analysis research.
</Info>

---

## Step 2: Apply Filter Menu

To transition from the sample data to actionable competitive intelligence, you must configure your data parameters using the filter panel.

![How To Use Insight 2](/images/how-to-use-insight-2.png)

Click the **Filter** button located in the top right corner of the dashboard to open the side panel and set the following parameters:

![How To Use Insight 2 2](/images/how-to-use-insight-2.2.png)

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Date Range">
    Select the specific start and end months for your historical analysis.

    <img
      src="/images/date-range.png"
      alt="Date Range"
      title="Date Range"
      className="mx-auto"
      style={{ width:"49%" }}
    />
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Location">
    Narrow down the competitor data to specific Provinces, Cities, or Districts to understand regional strategies.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Dimension (Brand & Industry)">
    This is where you select your target competitors based on the Playlog Taxonomy. You can search for and select specific individual **Brands** (e.g., specific instant noodle brands), overarching **Industries**, or narrower **Sub-Industries** (see Dimension Terminology).

    ![Dimension](/images/dimension.png)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Specifications">
    Filter the results by specific inventory types, such as filtering strictly for Digital Out-of-Home (**DOOH**) or conventional Out-of-Home (**OOH**) screens (see Specifications, Type & Category).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Once your parameters are set, click **Apply Filter**. The dashboard will instantly populate with real market data based on your criteria.

## Step 3: Start Exploring the Data & Grouping

With your filters applied, you can now explore the competitor analysis across the Coverage, Spending, Performance, and Ads Content tabs.

![How To Use Insight 3](/images/how-to-use-insight-3.png)

To manipulate how this filtered data is aggregated and visualized without resetting your main filters, utilize the **"Insights grouped by"** control bar at the top left of the dashboard. This allows you to pivot the data instantly using two distinct toggles:

<Accordion title="Grouping by Dimension (The First Toggle)">
  This setting dictates the business classification hierarchy used in the charts.

  ![Grouping Dimension](/images/grouping-dimension.png)

  **Industry: **Aggregates data by the highest-level market category. Metrics like "Total Brand" will change to **"Total Industry"**, providing a macro-view of the sector. 

  **Sub-industry:** Provides a focused view by grouping data into specific product categories (e.g., "Instant Food"). 

  **Brand:** The most specific view, grouping data by individual competitor brands for direct, head-to-head market share comparisons.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Grouping by Location (The Second Toggle)">
  This setting controls the geographical granularity, updating all location-based charts (e.g., Coverage and Spending distributions).

  ![Grouping Location](/images/grouping-location.png)

  **Province:** Rolls up the data to a macro-regional level. Charts will update to show aggregated totals like **"Ads By Province"**. 

  **City:** Provides a mid-level breakdown, displaying metrics based on specific cities or regencies. 

  **District:** Offers the most granular, localized view available. Charts will update to **"Ads By District"**, allowing you to analyze footprint in highly specific neighborhoods. 
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  **Strategic Tip:** Combine these groupings to answer specific business questions rapidly. For example, setting the grouping to **\[Industry\] in \[Province\]** provides a high-level overview of regional sector investment, while **\[Brand\] in \[District\]** pinpoints the exact hyper-local footprint of a specific competitor.
</Tip>
