## Step 4: Enter the OTP Code

    Enter the 4-digit OTP code that was sent to your email address.

    ![Enter OTP](/images/documentation/access/login/login-4.png)

    <Note>
      Please check your email's **Inbox** or **Spam/Junk** folder for the OTP code. If you still have not received the code, you can click the **"Click to resend"** link on the screen.
    </Note>
