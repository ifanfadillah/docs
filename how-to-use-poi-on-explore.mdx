---
title: "How to use POI"
---

Using the Point of Interest (POI) feature allows you to filter billboard inventories based on their proximity to specific real-world locations. Follow these steps to apply POI filters to your campaign planning.

## Step 1: Select a Specific City

Before you can access the POI tools, you must narrow down your geographic search.

Open the **Location** filter panel on the left side of the screen.

![How To Use Poi 1 1](/images/how-to-use-poi-1-1.png)

Select a specific city (e.g., _Jakarta Barat_).

![How To Use Poi 1 2](/images/how-to-use-poi-1.2.png)

The map will automatically zoom in to display the boundaries and inventories for that selected city and Click "Show Billboard".

![How To Use Poi 1 3](/images/how-to-use-poi-1.3.png)

<Info>
  Important Note: The POI button is disabled by default on the national view. A tooltip will remind you: "POI button available after you select a city". If you clear your city selection later, your POI configurations will be reset.
</Info>

---

## Step 2: Open the POI Panel

Once your city is selected, look to the floating toolbar on the right side of the map interface.

Click the **POI icon** (the map pin with a radius indicator) to open the Point of Interest settings panel.

![How To Use Poi 2](/images/how-to-use-poi-2.png)

## Step 3: Define Targeting Logic and Select Categories

In the POI panel, you will define exactly what environments you want to target.

**Choose your logic:** Decide whether you want to **Include** (only show billboards near these places) or **Exclude** (hide billboards near these places). You can use both simultaneously.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Default (No POI Settings)">
    When you first select a city, the map displays the default state where no proximity filters are applied. The map shows all available billboard inventories within your selected city boundaries. No radius zones are drawn during this state, giving you a complete, unfiltered view of the market.

    ![Default No POI](/images/Default-No-POI.png)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Include Only">
    Use this setting when your campaign must be placed near specific environments. For example, your media brief might require finding billboards in Jakarta Pusat that are strictly within a 1 km radius of Colleges, Libraries, and Malls. Once applied, the map will draw blue circular zones around your selected POIs based on the radius you set. The platform will then filter the map to only show billboards that successfully fall inside these blue zones, hiding all irrelevant inventories.

    ![Include Only](/images/Include-Only.png)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Exclude Only">
    Use this setting when your campaign needs to avoid certain environments, such as competitor storefronts or irrelevant demographic areas. For instance, you might want to find billboards in Jakarta Pusat that are at least 700 meters away from any Malls. When configured, the map will draw red circular zones around your selected POIs. The platform will immediately hide any billboards that fall inside these red zones, leaving only the inventories that are safely outside your restricted areas.

    ![Exclude Only](/images/Exclude-Only.png)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Include & Exclude">
    Use this setting for highly precise, laser-targeted media planning that combines both requirements. As an example, you might need to search for billboards that are within a 500-meter radius of Colleges, but simultaneously at least 700 meters away from any Malls. The map will draw both blue zones for your included categories and red zones for your excluded categories. To remain visible on your map, a billboard must satisfy both conditions: it must be located inside a blue zone while remaining completely outside of any red zones.

    ![Include & Exclude](/images/Include-&-Exclude.png)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

**Select POI Categories:** Click the search bar/dropdown under your chosen logic. A multi-level menu will appear.

![How To Use Poi 3](/images/how-to-use-poi-3.png)

Check the boxes next to the specific industries or sub-categories you want to target (e.g., expanding _Bank and Financial Institution_ and checking _ATM_ or _Bank Branch_). You can select multiple categories at once (see POI v2 (New)).

![How To Use Poi 3 2](/images/how-to-use-poi-3.2.png)

You also can adjust this from a minimum of **0 meters** up to a maximum of **1000 meters**.
