---
title: "Coverage"
---

The **Coverage** module is designed to provide comprehensive visibility into the reach, geographical / placement distribution, and volume of your competitors' Out-of-Home (OOH) and Digital Out-of-Home (DOOH) campaigns.

This page breaks down every section and chart available within the Coverage tab to help you interpret the data effectively.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Total Industries">
    The total count of unique, high-level market sectors (Industries) currently represented in your data view, based on the filters you have applied.

    ![Coverage Total Industries](/images/coverage-total-industries.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Total Brand">
    The total number of unique competitor brands currently active within your filtered dataset.

    ![Coverage Total Brand](/images/coverage-total-brand.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Total Billboards">
    The total number of unique physical inventory locations (such as individual digital screens or static conventional boards) being utilized by the selected campaigns.

    ![Coverage Total Billboard](/images/coverage-total-billboard.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Total Ads">
    The absolute total volume of individual advertisement creatives or ad plays running across all the identified inventory.

    ![Coverage Total Ads](/images/coverage-total-ads.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unique Billboard Per City">
    This chart illustrates the physical spread of the campaigns. It displays the total number of distinct advertising locations (billboards or screens) active in each city. The stacked bars use color coding to break down how much of that local inventory is **Conventional** (traditional static boards) versus **Digital** (DOOH screens).

    ![Coverage Unique Billboard Per City](/images/coverage-unique-billboard-per-city.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ads By City">
    While the previous chart shows physical locations, this chart measures campaign intensity. It displays the total volume of individual advertisements running in each city. For example, a city might have a low number of unique billboards but a high number of ads if those screens are highly utilized digital boards playing multiple creatives. This metric is also segmented by Conventional and Digital formats.

    ![Coverage Ads By City](/images/coverage-ads-by-city.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Brand Comparison">
    This pie chart provides a clear, high-level summary of the overall advertising market share among your selected competitors. It calculates the percentage distribution of total ads, allowing you to instantly see which brand is dominating the landscape within your specified filters.

    ![Coverage Brand Comparasion](/images/coverage-brand-comparasion.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ads By Brands">
    This stacked bar chart ranks the specific brands based on their total advertising volume. Importantly, it reveals each brand's specific media strategy by showing the exact breakdown of how many of their ads are deployed on Conventional inventory versus Digital inventory.

    ![Coverage Ads By Brands](/images/coverage-ads-by-brands.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Billboard Coverage by City">
    This section presents a detailed heatmap matrix. It displays the exact numerical count of physical billboards utilized by each brand (listed on the Y-axis) across different cities (listed on the X-axis).

    ![Coverage Billboard By City](/images/coverage-billboard-by-city.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Brand SOV (Share of Voice)">
    This 100% stacked bar chart illustrates historical dominance over time. It shows the Share of Voice (percentage of total advertising presence) for the selected brands on a month-by-month basis. This visualization is crucial for understanding how the competitive landscape shifts over time, revealing when specific brands launched aggressive campaigns or pulled back their spending.

    ![Coverage Brand Sov](/images/coverage-brand-sov.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Brand SOV by City">
    Similar to the previous chart, this is a 100% stacked bar chart, but it focuses on geographical dominance rather than time. It breaks down the Share of Voice within each specific city.

    ![Coverage Brand Sov By City](/images/coverage-brand-sov-by-city.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ads by Timeframe">
    This line chart tracks the trajectory of active ad volume over time for each selected brand, helping you identify campaign seasonality, momentum, and active periods.

    ![Coverage Ads By Months](/images/coverage-ads-by-months.png)

    <Info>
      By default, this chart displays data every **month** to show granular fluctuations. However, you can dynamically adjust the time aggregation using the dropdown menu located in the top right corner of the chart card. This allows you to view the data from different strategic perspectives:
    </Info>

    ![Coverage Ads By Months Filter](/images/coverage-ads-by-months-filter.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monthly Billboard Coverage">
    This section provides a highly detailed, tabular view of how billboard inventory is utilized month over month. It functions as a heatmap matrix where the color intensity of the blue cells instantly highlights periods of high activity. Beyond a high-level summary, this table is completely interactive, allowing you to drill down into the exact footprint of a competitor's campaign.

    ![Coverage Monthly Billboard 1](/images/coverage-monthly-billboard-1.png)

    <Accordion title="How to use the Expandable Hierarchy?">
      <Steps>
        <Step title="Brand Level (Default View)">
          Initially, the table lists the selected competitor brands on the left axis, showing their total active billboard count for each month across the top axis.

          ![Coverage Monthly Billboard](/images/coverage-monthly-billboard.png)
        </Step>
        <Step title="Location Level">
          Click the **plus (\+)** icon next to any brand name to expand its row. This reveals a secondary level showing exactly which **Cities** (or Provinces/Districts, depending on your master grouping settings) that brand was active in during those specific months.

          ![Coverage Monthly Billboard 2](/images/coverage-monthly-billboard-2.png)
        </Step>
        <Step title="Specific Billboard Level">
          Click the **plus (\+)** icon next to a specific city name to drill down even further. This reveals the most granular data available: the exact **Billboard Names** or street locations where the brand's advertisements were placed in that particular month.

          ![Coverage Monthly Billboard 3](/images/coverage-monthly-billboard-3.png)
        </Step>
      </Steps>
    </Accordion>
  </Step>
  <Step title="SES (Socioeconomic Status) by Brand">
    Understanding _where_ an ad is placed also tells you _who_ is likely seeing it. This section estimates the demographic profile of the audience exposed to the competitor's campaign based on their inventory locations.

    ![Coverage Ses](/images/coverage-ses.png)

    A grouped horizontal bar chart that provides a breakdown of the estimated audience's economic class. It segments the audience into tiers such as **Upper A, Upper B, Middle A, Middle B, Lower A, and Lower B**. By analyzing this chart, you can deduce the specific purchasing power and demographic segment a competitor is actively targeting. For example, you can easily identify if a brand is heavily skewed towards premium, high-income locations or if they are executing a mass-market strategy.
  </Step>
</Steps>
