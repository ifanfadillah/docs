title: "Download Insight"
---

The **Download** feature allows you to export the data, metrics, and charts you are currently viewing on the Insight (or Shortlist) dashboard into a professional, presentation-ready PowerPoint (`.pptx`) format.

Here are the steps to download your document:

## Step 1: Click the Download Button

In the top right corner of the dashboard page, locate and click the **Download** button (the downward-pointing arrow icon situated next to the Filter button).

![Download Insight 1](/images/download-insight-1.png)

---

## Step 2: Select a Presentation Template

Once the button is clicked, a **Download Document** pop-up modal will appear on your screen.

![Download Insight 2](/images/download-insight-2.png)

---

## Step 3: Click Download

After selecting your desired template, click the purple **Download** button at the bottom of the modal to begin processing and saving the presentation file to your device.

![Download Insight 3](/images/download-insight-3.png)
