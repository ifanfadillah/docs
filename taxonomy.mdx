title: "Taxonomy"
---

This page defines the fundamental classification standards and rules applied to the Playlog Insight (Taxonomy V1). Adhering to these rules ensures data consistency and accuracy across the Insight module, enabling reliable competitor analysis.

These classifications are applied through strict manual entry processes to ensure that what is shown in the advertisement is accurately represented in the database.

## Industry Category & IDs

| ID | Industry Name | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **01** | **Food & Grocery** | Food, food ingredients (both ready-to-eat and for cooking), and food products for household needs. |

## Sub Industry Name & IDs
### 01 - Food & Grocery

| ID | Sub Industry Name | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **01.01** | **Bakery & Baking Products** | Ingredients for making bread and cakes (and all other accessories) in solid and liquid forms. Examples: Live yeast, Raisins, TBM/Ovallet, Food Coloring, Cake Essence, Cake Flour. |
| **01.02** | **Breakfast Food** | Food for breakfast including accompaniments to be eaten with plain bread, excluding margarine. Examples: Bread, Jam, Sprinkles (Meisjes), Cereal, Cereal Milk. |
| **01.03** | **Snacks (Include Biscuits, Cookies, Chips)** | Light meals or snacks usually eaten as a refreshment. Examples: Cake, Layer Cake (Lapis Legit), Biscuits, Nuts, Wafers, Sunflower seeds (Kwaci), Popcorn, Dodol. Also includes side dishes for main meals (some ready-to-eat, some need cooking). Examples: Meat floss (Abon), Chips (kerupuk, emping, etc.). |
| **01.04** | **Confectionary Food** | Snacks made predominantly from cocoa/chocolate beans, usually in bar/rod or round shapes, sometimes with added nuts, biscuits, or wafers as a complement. Examples: Chocolate Bar, Chocolate Wafer. Includes all candies of various flavors (sweet, spicy, sour) including chewing gum, chocolate-flavored candies, and mouth-freshening candies. Examples: Candy, Chewing Gum. |
| **01.05** | **Cooking & Flavour Oil** | Food ingredients intended for frying food (in liquid form), produced from various sources (coconut, corn, etc.). Examples: Cooking Oil, Sunflower Oil, Corn Oil, Vegetable Oil, Coconut Oil, etc. Includes food ingredients used as flavor enhancers, typically for salad dressings. Examples: Mayonnaise, Salad Dressing, French Dressing, Thousand Island Dressing. |
| **01.06** | **Seasoning & Condiments** | Food seasonings added to food as flavor enhancers (whether solid, liquid, powder, or flour) including soy sauce and sauces (chili or tomato). Examples: Chili, Soy Sauce, Bouillon, Bouillon Cubes, Salt (MSG). |
| **01.07** | **Fresh & Frozen Food** | Food derived from fresh, dried, or frozen fruits or vegetables that do not go through canning or other processes. Examples: Fresh Fruit, Fresh Vegetable. |
| **01.08** | **Canned Foods** | Food derived from fresh, dried, or frozen fruits or vegetables packaged in special cans. Examples: Canned Fruit, vegetables, etc. Includes processed or manufactured food (meat, fish, fruit, etc.) packaged in cans or special containers. Examples: Canned Fish/Meat, Sausage, Corned beef, etc. |
| **01.09** | **Instant Food** | Fast food that must be cooked first in a short time or just mixed with hot water. Hard or solid before cooking and soft after cooking. Examples: Noodles, Soup, Macaroni, Spaghetti, Instant Chicken Porridge. |
| **01.10** | **Diet & Health Foods** | Food with a special formula (low calorie) intended for those trying to reduce or prevent excess weight (such as excess cholesterol or blood sugar levels, etc.), maintain body health, stability, and immunity. Examples: Diet Sugar/Low Calorie Sweetener, Diet Cookies, Diet Food, protein food, grainy food. |
| **01.11** | **Range of Food Products** | This category is intended for creative ads that simultaneously display various categories within one creative. |
| **01.12** | **Other Food Products** | Other types of Food Products that are not included in the sub-industries above. |
