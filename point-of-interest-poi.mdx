title: "What is POI"
---

In the Out-of-Home (OOH) advertising industry, location is everything. The **Point of Interest (POI)** in the Playlog platform provides users with a powerful geographic targeting tool that goes beyond simple city or district filtering.

A "Point of Interest" refers to any specific, real-world location that attracts a particular demographic or signifies a specific environment—such as shopping malls, universities, office buildings, hospitals, or transit hubs.

---

## The Purpose of the POI Feature

The POI feature is designed to help media buyers estimate audience intent and behavior based on the surrounding environment of a billboard. By understanding what physical structures surround an inventory asset, you can ensure your campaign reaches the exact audience you want.

**Key benefits include:**

- **Contextual Relevance:** Place an ad for a new sports drink exclusively on billboards within a 500-meter radius of gyms and sports arenas.
- **Competitor Conquesting:** Exclude billboards that are located right next to a competitor's storefront, or purposefully target those exact areas to capture their foot traffic.
- **Granular Audience Targeting:** Drill down into a multi-level taxonomy to find highly specific environments (e.g., placing ads near ATMS vs. corporate bank branches).

---

## How POI Works in Playlog

The POI feature is deeply integrated into the Playlog **Explore** map. Once a user selects a specific city, they unlock the ability to overlay POI data onto their media planning map.

![Poi 1](/images/poi-1.png)

Users interact with the POI feature using three main components:

1. **Targeting Logic:** Choosing whether to **Include** (only show screens near a POI), **Exclude** (hide screens near a POI), or combine both.
2. **Taxonomy Selection:** Browsing through our structured POI categories to select the exact industries or sub-categories relevant to the campaign.
3. **Radius Configuration:** Using a dynamic slider to define the geographic boundary of the POI influence, ranging from exactly 0 meters up to 1,000 meters.

By mastering the POI feature, media buyers transition from buying broad geographic areas to executing highly strategic, intent-driven media placements.
