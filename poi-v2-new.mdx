## Introducing POI V2

To enable more precise and competitive inventory activation, Playlog has upgraded its Point of Interest (POI) targeting capabilities. Because POI data is foundational for core media buying functions including inventory targeting, campaign planning, and audience segmentation. This update ensures your geographic targeting is built on the most reliable spatial data available.

---

## Key Enhancements over V1

While the legacy V1 data provided a baseline for location filtering, it faced limitations in coverage, accuracy, and taxonomy depth. POI V2 addresses these challenges by introducing:

- **Enhanced Coverage & Accuracy:** A significantly expanded and consistently updated dataset that gives you a more reliable picture of the real-world environment surrounding your target screens.
- **Granular, Multi-Level Taxonomy:** A newly restructured taxonomy that allows for much deeper, highly specific location filtering (e.g., drilling down from broad commercial zones to specific types of retail stores).
- **Improved Data Consistency:** Resolves the quality issues inherent in web-scraped data, ensuring your inclusion and exclusion parameters hit the exact audience intent you are aiming for.

<Note>
  The standard POI filtering mechanics, Include, Exclude, and Include & Exclude, remain the same. However, the data powering these selections is now significantly more robust.
</Note>
