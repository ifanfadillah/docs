## Industry

The primary, highest level of classification for the product or service displayed in an advertisement. This represents the broadest market category.

---

## Sub-Industry

A derivative classification that falls under a broader Industry umbrella. It groups products or services that share similar core functions or foundational raw materials.

---

## Advertiser

**Advertiser:** The parent company or corporate entity that owns the Brand. **Data Sourcing Rule:** If the company name is explicitly stated in the ad creative, it is extracted directly. If the ad creative lacks this information, the data entry process requires looking up the parent company via external search (e.g., Google) or obtaining the information directly from the client (the brand holder).

---

## Brand

The overarching brand name of the product being advertised. The platform strictly extracts the "Umbrella Brand" (Top Brand) as the primary brand identifier.

---

## Sub-Brand (Detailed Naming Conventions)

The **Sub-Brand** is the specific product name, which includes any variant names. The naming convention follows these strict rules and edge cases:

- **Packaging Dictates Naming:** The sub-brand or product name must perfectly match the branding physically printed on the packaging (box, pack, plastic bag, can, bottle, etc.) shown in the advertisement's visual.
- **Handling Missing Packaging (New Sub-Brands):** If a new ad airs without showing physical packaging, the sub-brand is temporarily named based on the visual creative. However, if the product later airs an ad showing its packaging or logo, the historical sub-brand record will be retroactively adjusted to match the official packaging.
- **Handling Missing Packaging (Existing Sub-Brands):** If an ad airs without packaging or logos, but the product has been advertised previously _with_ visible packaging, the system will apply the historical, verified packaging name to the new ad.
- **Flavor as a Function (Elevated Sub-Brands):** In specific cases, a flavor is elevated to a sub-brand if that flavor implies a distinct efficacy, use-case, or characteristic that differentiates it from previous products (based on manufacturer claims).
  - _Examples:_ HiLo Kacang Hijau, Kapal Api Susu, Indocafe Kopi Ginseng.
- **Unified Subjective Flavors:** If a flavor variant does not indicate a specific absolute ingredient but rather a relative/subjective taste, and it is visually presented as a unified name with the product, it is recorded entirely as the sub-brand.
  - _Examples:_ Kopi Kapal Api Rasa Mantap, Nescafe Pas!
- **Tobacco Products Special Rule:** Cigarette sub-products are consolidated and recorded under a single cigarette sub-brand name if the sub-product is displayed in the ad, alongside the mandatory Government Warning regarding the dangers of smoking.
