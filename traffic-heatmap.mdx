title: "Traffic Heatmap"
---

The **Traffic Heatmap** is a powerful visual overlay tool within the Playlog Explore map. It projects traffic condition data directly onto the road networks surrounding billboard inventories.

By analyzing traffic patterns, media buyers can strategically select billboards in areas prone to slower movement or congestion, which naturally increases audience dwell time and maximizes advertisement exposure (impressions).

## How to Activate the Traffic Heatmap

To prevent overwhelming data rendering, the Traffic Heatmap is disabled on the national overview. You must narrow your search to a specific region first.

**Step 1: Open the Location filter panel on the left side of the Explore screen.**

![How To Use Traffic Heatmap 1](/images/how-to-use-traffic-heatmap-1.png)

**Step 2: Select a specific City (e.g., _Jakarta_).**

![How To Use Traffic Heatmap 2](/images/how-to-use-traffic-heatmap-2.png)

**Step 3: Click the Show Billboard button to apply your filter and zoom into the city.**

![How To Use Traffic Heatmap 3](/images/how-to-use-traffic-heatmap-3.png)

**Step 4: On the map's floating toolbar, click the Traffic Heatmap icon to toggle the data overlay on or off.**

![How To Use Traffic Heatmap 4](/images/how-to-use-traffic-heatmap-4.png)

![How To Use Traffic Heatmap 5](/images/how-to-use-traffic-heatmap-5.png)

## Traffic Condition Indicators

Once activated, the map's road networks will be highlighted in different colors. You can hover over specific roads to see detailed descriptions. The heatmap utilizes a five-tier legend to classify traffic density:

![Screenshot 2026 05 18 120233](/images/Screenshot-2026-05-18-120233.png)

**Light:** Almost empty roads with minimal traffic.

**Low:** Light traffic, allowing smooth and easy movement.

**Moderate:** Moderate traffic, slight congestion but still moving well.

**Heavy:** Heavy traffic, noticeable congestion, and slower movement.

**Very Heavy:** Very heavy traffic, significant congestion, and slower travel speeds.

**Gridlock:** Extremely heavy traffic, severe congestion, and slow-moving or standstill conditions.

<Tip>
  **Media Planning Tip:** Billboards located near intersections or road segments frequently marked as _Heavy_ or _Very Heavy_ often yield better performance metrics due to the prolonged viewing time available to motorists and passengers.
</Tip>
